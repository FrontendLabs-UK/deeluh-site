Everything your firm needs in one platform.

Most firms run on a patchwork of separate tools — spreadsheets for matters, messaging apps for clients, notebooks for trust accounts, memory for compliance. Deeluh replaces the patchwork with one connected system built specifically for legal practice.

Client Messaging WhatsApp Secure communication with clients inside Deeluh.
Internal Communication Slack Collaborate with your legal team without switching platforms.
Knowledge Management Notion Store notes, legal knowledge, and firm documentation in one place.
Matter & Task Management ClickUp Manage legal matters, tasks, deadlines, and workflows.
Workflow Boards Trello Track legal workflows without separate project management tools.
Project Planning Asana Coordinate legal projects from a single workspace.

Seven modules. One firm. Everything connected.

Deeluh brings every essential part of legal practice into one connected platform. Instead of switching between disconnected software, every workflow lives together—from firm setup and matter management to billing, AI assistance, compliance and secure communication.

01

Firm Setup

Your firm, structured correctly, from day one.

Set up your firm structure and invite your team with the right permissions in under two minutes. Whether you're a sole practitioner or a multi-partner firm, Deeluh asks the right questions upfront so everything downstream—billing, approvals and access—is configured correctly from the start.

02

Matter Management

Every matter, organized the way your firm actually works.

Track every matter from intake to close in a Kanban view built specifically for legal work—not repurposed project management software. Parties, deadlines, documents, time entries and activity history all live together with every action recorded in a complete audit trail.

03

Client Portal

Give clients answers before they have to ask.

A secure branded portal where clients can monitor progress, upload documents, view upcoming milestones and see funds held on their behalf without calling your office for updates.

04

Billing & Payments

Get paid without the chase.

Track billable time automatically, generate invoices instantly and accept payments seamlessly. Built specifically for Nigerian legal practice with Naira-denominated billing.

05

Deeluh AI

An assistant, not a replacement.

Deeluh AI assists with legal drafting, document review, legal research and analysis while ensuring lawyers remain fully in control. Every result is transparent, reviewable and verifiable.

06

Team Management & Communication

Keep the conversation where the work happens.

Manage permissions, workloads and collaboration across your entire firm. Matter-specific communication keeps conversations attached to the work while WhatsApp Business integration keeps client communication connected.

07

Trust & Compliance Records

Built for professional-conduct review, not just bookkeeping.

Every financial activity is protected by a tamper-evident hash-chain audit trail together with strict separation of client funds, trust funds and operating funds.

Hash-chain audit trail Tamper-evident transaction history Three-pool separation Client, trust and operating funds remain completely separated
