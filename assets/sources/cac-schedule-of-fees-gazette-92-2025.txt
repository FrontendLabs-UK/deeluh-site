EXTRACT FOR VERIFICATION — Corporate Affairs Commission, New Schedule of Fees.

Source: S.I. No. 6, Federal Republic of Nigeria Official Gazette No. 92, Vol. 112,
Lagos, 29th May 2025; the schedule itself is dated 31st October 2024 and signed by
the Minister of Industry, Trade and Investment.
Retrieved 23 November 2026 from the Commission's own site and converted to text.

Why it is in this repo: /tools/cac-registration-fees/ publishes these figures, and
scripts/check-tool-fees.py checks the page against this file on every push. Without a
committed copy of the source, nothing would notice a mistyped fee on a public page.
It is a government instrument, reproduced here for that check.
