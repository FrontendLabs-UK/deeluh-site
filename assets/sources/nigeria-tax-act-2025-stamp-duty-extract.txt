EXTRACT FOR VERIFICATION — Nigeria Tax Act, 2025 (2025 No. 7), stamp duty provisions.

Retrieved 23 November 2026 as a PDF of the Act and converted to text. Only the parts
/tools/stamp-duty-nigeria/ relies on are reproduced: the obligation to stamp, the lease
provision, the share-capital charge, and the rows of the duty table the page publishes.

WHY IT IS IN THIS REPO: the page publishes rates from this Act, and without a committed
copy of the source nothing would notice a mistyped percentage on a public page.

ONE CELL IS GENUINELY MISSING FROM THE SOURCE, AND THE PAGE SAYS SO. The lease row reads
"Up to 7 years = 0.78%. Above 7" and then stops: the continuation is absent from the PDF's
text layer in this copy, in both layout and raw extraction, and the string "years =" occurs
exactly once in the whole document. So the rate for leases ABOVE seven years is NOT sourced
here and is marked "being confirmed" on the page rather than filled in from commentary.

                building, shall be subject to duty on grant of a lease or sublease, or the assignment
                of a lease, and shall be charged with the same duty as if it were an actual lease
                made for the term and consideration mentioned in the agreement, provided
                that lease agreements for which the annual value is less than N10,000,000 or
                10 times the annual minimum wage, whichever is higher, shall not be chargeable
                with any duty under Chapter Five of this Act.
