Whatever your firm's structure, Deeluh fits.

Deeluh's data model handles the role permissions, co-approval rules, and regulatory requirements specific to how your practice is actually organised — not a generic one-size-fits-all setup.

Sole Practitioner

You are the firm.

One login, one desk, everything Deeluh ships — priced for one. The card below is your whole workspace: intake in, billing out, no approval layers standing between the two.

Sole Proprietorship

You own the firm. Associates work under you.

Role permissions built in from the start, with billing per seat as your team grows. The hierarchy below is live in your workspace on day one — you keep oversight, associates get exactly the access you set.

Partnership

Decisions that need every partner's sign-off, actually need every partner's sign-off.

Co-approval is built directly into firm workflows. Upload your partnership deed and Deeluh enforces the approval paths below automatically — nothing executes until both partners have signed.

Multi-Partner Firm

Distinct practice groups. One firm-wide system.

Department separation, associateship isolation, complete audit trails and a shared knowledge vault — practice groups stay walled off from each other, but everything still lands in one hash-secured record.

Legal Department

Built for in-house teams, not law firms.

Track contracts, compliance and legal requests across the business without the billing and trust-accounting workflows built for firms. Every request moves through a clear lifecycle, end to end.

Enterprise

Complex structures. Custom configuration.

Dedicated data residency, custom compliance workflows and enterprise configuration for legal organisations operating across multiple offices, entities or jurisdictions — all routed through a single governance layer.
