What Deeluh does

Everything your firm needs in one platform.

Most firms run on a patchwork of separate tools, spreadsheets for matters, messaging apps for clients, notebooks for trust accounts, memory for compliance. Deeluh replaces the patchwork with one connected system built specifically for legal practice.

01

Firm setup

Sets up your firm structure and invites your team with the right permissions. Workspace ready in under two minutes.

02

Matter management

Kanban by stage. Parties, time entries, deadlines, and documents in one view. Every action audit-trailed to the minute.

03

Client portal

KA secure, branded space where your clients track their matter, see what's next, upload documents, and view funds held on their behalf, without ever calling to ask for an update.

04

Billing & payments

Track time automatically. Generate invoices instantly. Accept payments seamlessly. Get paid without the chase.

05

Deeluh AI

An assistant built into the platform that helps lawyers draft contracts, review documents, and conduct research, under lawyer review at every step. Judgment stays with the lawyer, always.

06

Team management & communication

Manage roles, permissions, and workload across your firm. Per-matter channels keep discussion where the work happens, with WhatsApp Business integration for client-facing threads.

07

Trust & compliance records

Hash-chain audit trail. Three-pool separation of client, trust, and subscription funds. Built for professional-conduct review.
