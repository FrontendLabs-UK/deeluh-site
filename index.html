What Deeluh does

One platform for the whole firm.

Most firms run on a patchwork of separate tools: spreadsheets for matters, messaging apps for clients and memory for compliance. Deeluh replaces the patchwork with a platform built for legal practice.

01 Live

Firm set-up

Set up your firm structure and invite your team with the right permissions.

02 Live

Matter management

See every matter on a board by stage, with its parties, time entries, deadlines and documents in one view. Changes are recorded in the firm's audit trail.

03 Live

Client portal

A secure private link for each matter. Your clients see what you share, sign and upload what you ask for, view their invoices and message your team, without calling to ask for an update.

04 Live

Billing and payments

Record time against each matter, raise invoices line by line and send reminders when they fall due. Clients pay by bank transfer, or by card through your firm's own Paystack or Stripe account.

05 Live

Deeluh AI

An assistant built into the platform that helps lawyers redraft text, review contracts and ask questions of their firm's own documents, under lawyer review at every step. Judgment stays with the lawyer, always.

06 Live

Team management and communication

Manage roles, permissions and workload across your firm. Discussion stays inside each matter, and clients message your team through the client portal. WhatsApp Business is in pilot.

07 Live

Compliance records

A tamper-evident, hash-chained audit trail of firm actions, designed to support professional-conduct review.

Deeluh handles the role permissions and regulatory requirements that come with your firm's structure.

Role-based permissions, a firm-wide document vault and a tamper-evident audit trail, with confidential matters visible only to the people working on them.
