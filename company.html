About Deeluh




Our Mission

Deeluh exists to give every law firm, from solo practitioners to full-service firms, an operating system built around how legal work actually happens, so that lawyers can spend their time practising law rather than managing the software meant to support them.

Our Values

We believe that judgment should always stay with the lawyer, and that technology's job is to remove friction rather than to replace professional judgment, which is why every matter-facing feature we build ships under strict lawyer-review boundaries. We also believe in building for how firms actually run, rather than guessing at what a firm might need from the outside, so every workflow inside Deeluh is shaped by real legal practice rather than assumptions made at a distance from it. And because we're building for firms operating in a market that has too often been an afterthought for global software, we price in local currency from the outset, rather than adopting a dollar-denominated model built around an economy we don't serve.

Law firms today run on some of the most sophisticated professional judgment found in any industry, and yet that judgment is too often supported by some of the least sophisticated tools available to it: matters tracked in a spreadsheet, clients reached through a messaging app never designed for confidential communication, trust accounts reconciled by hand, and compliance held together largely by memory and good intentions. That gap doesn't only slow a firm down; it quietly costs both the client and the lawyer, because a firm can only grow as far as one person's memory can stretch, and no lawyer should have to carry an entire practice's operations in their head simply to keep that practice running.

About Frontendlabs

Frontendlabs is a technology studio that builds products for both funded startups and pre-seed ventures, bringing more than fifteen years of combined team experience to that work, alongside a track record that includes reaching four million people worldwide and delivering £120 million in client value through past acquisitions. Deeluh is Frontendlabs' answer to a problem we saw up close within the legal industry, and we've approached building it with the same philosophy we bring to every product we take on: get to market, get it right, and stay for the long run.
