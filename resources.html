Blog

Our blog will cover the day-to-day work of running a law firm, from managing client expectations and structuring billing to meeting compliance obligations and deciding when and how to bring technology into your practice. We write for lawyers who are building a firm, not only practising in one, and we aim to make every piece useful rather than promotional.

The first articles will be published soon. In the meantime, follow Deeluh on LinkedIn to see them as they appear.

Guides

Where the blog answers timely questions, our guides go deeper: longer pieces written to be kept and used as reference. Expect practical guidance on structuring a firm for growth, getting trust accounting right and understanding what the NDPA requires of a law firm in Nigeria.

Our first guides are being written. Follow Deeluh on LinkedIn to hear when they are published.
