Free legal tools

Working tools for the questions Nigerians actually search for, free and with no sign-up. Each one says where its figures come from and when they were last checked — and where we could not source something, it says that instead of guessing.

Why the sourcing is on every page. Most of what is published about Nigerian fees and duties is out of date or contradicts itself: CAC fees are quoted at three different numbers, and stamp duty rates written before January 2026 describe a law that has been replaced. So each page names its instrument, and anything we have not confirmed is marked as still being confirmed rather than filled in from somebody’s summary. General information, not legal advice.

  • Quit notice (Lagos)

    How much notice a landlord must give, by tenancy type — and the four areas of Lagos where the Tenancy Law does not apply at all. Generates the notice.

    Section 13, Tenancy Law of Lagos State 2011

  • Tenancy agreement (Lagos)

    A tenancy agreement you fill in and print, with a note on each clause — and the stamp duty answer, which for most Lagos tenancies is that none is payable.

    Tenancy Law 2011 · Nigeria Tax Act 2025

  • CAC registration fees

    What the Commission charges to register a business name, a company, an NGO, an LLP or an LP — including what “or part thereof” does to the share-capital fee.

    CAC New Schedule of Fees, Gazette No. 92 of 2025

  • Stamp duty

    The rates that replaced the old schedule in January 2026, who pays each one, the 30-day deadline, and the exemption that takes most tenancies out entirely.

    Nigeria Tax Act 2025

  • Power of attorney

    A power of attorney you fill in, who must witness it, and why one touching land needs a lawyer rather than a template.

    Practitioners’ published commentary

  • Letter of administration (Lagos)

    What to gather when somebody dies without a will, what happens after you file, and how long each part takes.

    Lagos probate practice guidance
