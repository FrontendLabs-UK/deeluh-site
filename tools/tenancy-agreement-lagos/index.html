Tenancy agreement for Lagos

Fill it in, read the note beside each clause, print it, and have a lawyer look at it before anybody signs. The generic templates you can download elsewhere are not wrong, they are just not about Lagos — so they say nothing about the notice period your choice of term creates, the four areas the Tenancy Law does not cover, or the fact that most Lagos tenancies are exempt from stamp duty.

Last checked 23 November 2026. Legal points from the Tenancy Law of Lagos State 2011 and the Nigeria Tax Act 2025. This is a starting point, not legal advice, and not a finished agreement. A tenancy agreement is a document somebody signs and is bound by; have a lawyer settle it for your property and your circumstances.

Fill in the agreement

Three Lagos points a generic template will not tell you

1. How you take the rent decides the notice you must give

Under section 13 of the Tenancy Law 2011, the notice needed to end a tenancy follows the kind of tenancy — which is generally decided by when rent is paid or demanded, not by what the agreement calls it. One month for a monthly tenant, three for a quarterly or half-yearly one, six for a yearly one. Taking rent yearly because it is convenient also commits you to six months’ notice. Our quit notice page works the periods out.

Section 13 applies only where the agreement is silent. If you state a notice period, that is what binds both of you — so state one deliberately or leave it out deliberately, but do not leave it to a clause somebody copied.

2. The law does not cover all of Lagos

Apapa, Ikeja GRA, Ikoyi and Victoria Island sit under the Recovery of Premises Law instead. If the property is in one of them, the notice regime is different and a clause drafted against the Tenancy Law may not do what you think. We have that from practitioners’ commentary rather than the face of the statute, so confirm it for the specific property.

3. Most Lagos tenancies owe no stamp duty at all

Section 135 of the Nigeria Tax Act 2025 exempts a lease agreement whose annual value is less than ₦10,000,000, or ten times the annual minimum wage, whichever is higher. So an ordinary tenancy is usually not chargeable — despite a great many pages still telling Nigerians they owe 6% on one. Above the threshold a lease of up to seven years is charged at 0.78%, payable by the tenant, and it must be stamped within 30 days of execution. The stamp duty page has the detail.

What each clause is for

The parties and the premises

Full names, and an address for each where notices can be served. Describe the premises so that nobody can argue about which flat — “Flat 3” in a building with two Flat 3s has started more disputes than any clause further down.

The term, and what happens at the end of it

A fixed term ends by effluxion of time and needs no notice to quit — but a landlord who wants possession must still serve a seven-day notice of intention to apply for it. A term that simply runs on becomes a periodic tenancy, with the notice period that follows from how rent is taken.

Rent, and the deposit

Say the amount, the period it covers, and when it falls due. For a deposit, the clause that matters is not the amount but the conditions for its return and who decides whether they are met. A deposit clause that says only “refundable subject to damage” is an argument waiting to happen.

Repairs, use, alterations and subletting

The ordinary division is that the tenant keeps the inside in the state they got it, fair wear excepted, and the landlord keeps the structure. Alterations and subletting are usually made subject to the landlord’s written consent. The word doing the work in all three is whatever standard you set, so set one.

Quiet enjoyment

The landlord’s promise not to interfere with the tenant’s possession. It is short, it is standard, and leaving it out is noticed.

Signatures and witnesses

Both parties sign and date, each with a witness who gives their name, address and occupation. For anything touching an interest in land, take advice on execution and on whether more is needed — a tenancy agreement is not the same instrument as a deed, and the requirements are not the same either.

What this template is not

It is a residential tenancy starting point. It is not drafted for a commercial lease, a service apartment, a sublease, a rent-to-own arrangement, or anything involving an assignment of a lease or a transfer of an interest in land. It makes no provision for a managing agent, a service charge regime or a guarantor. If your facts include any of those, this is the wrong document to start from.

Where this comes from

  • Tenancy Law of Lagos State 2011, section 13 — notice periods, and that they apply where the agreement is silent
  • Nigeria Tax Act 2025, sections 126 and 135 and the duty table — the stamp duty position
  • Nigerian practitioners’ published commentary — the excluded areas, and that a 2025 bill to amend the Lagos tenancy rules has not become law
